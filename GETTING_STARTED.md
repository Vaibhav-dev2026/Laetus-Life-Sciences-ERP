# GETTING STARTED — Your First Day With the App

This guide is for you, the business owner — no technical knowledge needed. It uses the exact
button and menu names you'll actually see on screen.

---

## A. Logging in for the first time

1. Open the app's web address in Chrome or Edge — on your computer, tablet, or phone. (Ask
   whoever set this up for you for the exact address — it will look like
   `https://something.vercel.app`.)
2. Enter the email and password that were set up for you when the app was installed. This is
   the only login the app needs — there is no separate sign-up page, and no other account to
   remember or share.
3. If you ever want to change your password: click **My Account** in the left-hand menu (under
   "Administration"), then use the **Change Password** section — enter your current password
   and your new one, and click **Update Password**.

---

## B. Setting up your company details (do this once, first)

1. In the left-hand menu, click **Company Settings** (under "Administration").
2. Enter your company name, address, GSTIN, PAN, bank account details, and upload your logo.
3. Save. These details now appear automatically on every invoice, purchase order, and report
   you print or download from now on — so it's worth getting this right before you create your
   first real bill.

---

## C. Setting up your master data (do this once, before your first real bill)

You need at least one Product, one Customer, and one Supplier before you can record a real
purchase or sale.

**Adding a Product:**
1. Click **Products** in the left menu.
2. Click **+ Add Product**.
3. Fill in the product name, HSN code, GST rate, pack size, MRP, and rates. Save.

**Adding a Customer** (a doctor, clinic, hospital, or medical store you sell to):
1. Click **Customers** in the left menu.
2. Click **+ Add Customer**.
3. Fill in the party name, mobile number, address, and GSTIN (if they have one). Save.

**Adding a Supplier** (who you buy stock from):
1. Click **Suppliers** in the left menu.
2. Click **+ Add Supplier**.
3. Fill in the company name, contact details, and GSTIN. Save.

---

## D. Your first real Purchase (stock coming in)

1. Click **Purchases** in the left menu, then **+ New Purchase**.
2. Select the Supplier, enter the purchase invoice number and date.
3. Add each product line: product, batch number, expiry date, quantity, and rate (PTS). The app
   calculates GST and totals automatically.
4. Click **Save**.
5. **To confirm it worked:** click **Batches** or **Inventory** in the left menu — your new
   batch and increased stock quantity should appear immediately.

---

## E. Your first real Sale / Bill (your daily task)

1. Click **Sales / Billing** in the left menu, then **+ New Invoice**.
2. Select the Customer.
3. Add each product line — the app will show you which batches are available to sell from,
   with stock and expiry shown so you pick the right one.
4. Click **Save**, or click **Save & Preview** if you want to see the invoice before finalizing.

**Printing, downloading, and previewing — the three buttons, explained:**
- **Print Invoice** — opens your browser/printer's print dialog directly with the invoice
  ready to print. Use this when you're printing on paper right now.
- **Download PDF** — saves the invoice as a PDF file to your device, so you can email it or
  keep a digital copy.
- The **📄 PDF** button (on report/list screens) opens a preview first, with a Download button
  inside — use this when you want to check it looks right before saving or printing.

---

## F. Getting paid (Receipts)

1. Click **Payments** in the left menu, then **+ Record Payment**.
2. Search for and select the customer — every customer you've ever created appears here,
   searchable by name, so picking the right one links the payment correctly to their account.
3. Enter the amount received and the payment mode (Cash, UPI, Bank Transfer, Cheque, Card).
4. Click **Save Payment**.
5. **To confirm it worked:** open **Outstanding** in the left menu (under "Accounts") — that
   customer's balance should now be reduced by the amount you just recorded.

---

## G. Your daily/weekly checks

- **Dashboard** (top of the left menu) — a quick daily summary: sales trend, top products, and
  outstanding at a glance.
- **Outstanding** (under "Accounts") — who owes you money, and for how long.
- **Inventory** / **Batches** (under "Masters") — what's running low or close to expiry.

---

## H. Month-end / GST time

1. Click **GST Reports** in the left menu — this takes you to **GSTR-1**. **ITC Reconciliation**
   and **GSTR-3B** are nearby tabs/links on the same GST section.
2. Set the date range for the month you want (e.g. "last month").
3. Click **Excel**, **CSV**, or the **📄 PDF** button to export that report and hand it to your
   CA/accountant.

**Important:** this app produces the exact reports (GSTR-1, GSTR-2/ITC-style, GSTR-3B) your CA
needs, in the format they're used to — but it does **not** itself file your GST return with the
government. You (or your CA) still upload these exported files to the official GST portal, the
same way it would work with any other billing software.

---

## I. Keeping your data safe

1. Click **Backup** in the left menu (under "Administration"), then click **+ Backup Now**
   whenever you want to trigger a fresh backup.
2. **Once a month, download that backup and save a copy somewhere outside the app** — email it
   to yourself, or save it to a USB drive. Think of it as a spare key: the app keeps your data
   safe on its own, but an extra copy costs you nothing and means you're never depending on any
   one single place.
3. **Reassurance:** your data lives permanently in the online database, not inside your web
   browser. Refreshing the page, closing the browser, restarting your computer, or logging in
   from a different phone or computer will **never** lose your data — as long as you log in
   with the same email and password every time.

---

## J. If something looks wrong

- **The app feels slow or doesn't respond the very first time you open it in the morning** —
  this is normal. Wait 30–60 seconds and try again; the free server "rests" when nobody's used
  it for a while and takes a moment to wake up. It will work normally after that first try.
- **A page shows an error message instead of your data** — refresh the page first. If it keeps
  happening, note down exactly what you were doing (which page, which button) before reaching
  out for help.
- **You're not sure if something saved correctly** — every save shows a confirmation message on
  screen; if you don't see one, it likely didn't save — try again rather than assuming it worked.
- For anything else, reach out to whoever set this app up for you, with a description of what
  you were trying to do and what happened instead.
